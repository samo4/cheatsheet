# About this

> **Draft.** I attended lectures by Prof. Dr. Miran Bürmen. As I tried to make sense of my notes (and notes from unnamed peer), I started to transcribe them and once I employed LLMs to help me, things quickly escalated. While I mostly stuck to the core content of Dr. Bürmen's lectures, I reordered things where I felt the flow warrants it, added explanations, examples and my own interpretations, and removed or downplayed parts I found less relevant. I also dragged in the Jordan canonical form that was aparently dropped from the course some years ago. The only thing I guarantee is that it's full of errors, and that most of them are not LLM hallucinations. Use at your own risk.

Pull requests are welcome: [https://github.com/samo4/lnas](https://github.com/samo4/lnas).
