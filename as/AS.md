# About this

> **Draft.** I attended lectures by Prof. Dr. Miran Bürmen. As I tried to make sense of my notes (and notes from unnamed peer), I started to transcribe them and once I employed LLMs to help me, things quickly escalated. While I mostly stuck to the core content of Dr. Bürmen's lectures, I reordered things where I felt the flow warrants it, added explanations, examples and my own interpretations, and removed or downplayed parts I found less relevant. The only thing I guarantee is that it's full of errors, and that most of them are not LLM hallucinations. Use at your own risk.

The course itself follows the proud tradition of European universities: mathematical rigor and a deep understanding of the underlying principles are emphasized over mere procedural knowledge. If you have the time, it's well worth the occasional rabbit hole to find elegant connections from different areas of mathematics and engineering. But spending time on derivations is a real opportunity cost. Ultimately the course gives you some tools on the road to controlling systems, but stops short of the "controlling" part.
